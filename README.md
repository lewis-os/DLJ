# DLJ — Danika Lewis Journal

> A living archive of curiosity, conversations, and meaningful moments — thoughts, discoveries, and stories from 2026, preserved as letters to the future.

DLJ is a personal-interest journal, not a formal research project or a news publication. It keeps the signals that make us pause: an idea, a conversation, a piece of technology, or a human question that may matter differently when viewed from the future.

Each entry preserves the source as faithfully as the available record permits, separates the source's claims from our interpretation, and closes with a **Letter from 2026**. The aim is not to predict perfectly. It is to leave an honest record of what caught our attention, what we understood, and what we were still asking.

## How DLJ is organized

```text
DLJ/
├── README.md
├── TIMELINE.md
├── entries/
│   └── 2026/
│       └── 2026-07-26-supernova-os.md
├── reflections/
│   └── weekly/
│       └── TEMPLATE.md
└── templates/
    └── ENTRY_TEMPLATE.md
```

- [`TIMELINE.md`](TIMELINE.md) is the chronological index.
- [`entries/`](entries/) contains individual journal entries grouped by year.
- [`reflections/weekly/`](reflections/weekly/) turns a week of separate signals into a wider reflection.
- [`templates/`](templates/) keeps the structure consistent without making the journal mechanical.

## Entry principles

1. **Preserve before interpreting.** Record the title, author, source, date, link, and available source text.
2. **Name uncertainty.** If metadata or technical evidence is unavailable, say so.
3. **Separate claim from fact.** A source author's statement remains an attributed claim until independently verified.
4. **Keep the human in the frame.** Ask who gains agency, who carries risk, and what kind of future the idea makes possible.
5. **Explain it plainly.** Every completed entry ends with an **ELI21** explanation: accurate, accessible, and free of unnecessary jargon.
6. **Write to the future.** Close with a dated letter that captures what 2026 could and could not yet see.

## Status vocabulary

- `captured` — the source and first observations are preserved
- `reflecting` — interpretation is still developing
- `revisit` — the entry has explicit questions or claims to check later
- `closed` — no further review is currently planned

## A note on evidence

DLJ entries are records of attention, not endorsements. Technical, scientific, political, or commercial claims belong to their original sources unless an entry explicitly cites independent evidence. Corrections and later understanding should be appended with dates rather than silently rewriting what 2026 believed.
